## Verdict

**Keep v2 live; do not revert.** It is more precise on every pool, and the wording fixes all held. But it trades away about 1.4 good items a day, concentrated in model releases and local-inference reports.

**Candidate fix:** `prompts/triage_v2_1.md` is v2 plus one Favor bullet. It says frontier and open-weight releases and hands-on local-inference reports with numbers are always in scope, with one pick per release. It is untested.
