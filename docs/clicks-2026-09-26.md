## What it says

- The model's scores are calibrated: open rate rises steadily with score. Picks scored 5-6 are rarely opened.
- Position matters a lot: the first pick is opened half the time, picks 7-10 under 12%. AI was always the first section, so part of AI's lead is position.
- Japan picks were opened 8% of the time (Japan Times 7% over 357 picks) — the old Japan mix wasn't what Ian wants, matching his "less geopolitics, more varied Japanese sources". World news: 2%.
- Local-AI practitioner sources lead (r/LocalLLaMA 42%, HN 37%), consistent with "follow people who publish their own work".
