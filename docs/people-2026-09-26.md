# People to follow — 2026-09-26

Ian's question: if well-informed people follow *people* rather than outlets, how do you find the best people in your fields? This is the method that worked, and the 36 feeds it added (8 lanes: local AI, research agents, Deleuze and AI, fitness and health science, Japanese thinkers, JDM trade, Kyberna, generalists). Every feed was verified on EVO-X2 (HTTP 200, recent posts) and checked post-by-post for paywalls; a skeptic pass re-verified each lane.

## How to find them

**Follow an endorsement graph, not a fame list.**

1. **Start with 5–10 seeds who make primary things**: they built the tool, ran the study or wrote the paper. Examples: Simon Willison and the llama.cpp tracker for local AI, 稲葉振一郎 for Japan, Dan Davies for VSM.
2. **Mine who the seeds point to.** What worked differed by field:
   - **Substack recommendation lists** were the best single signal for evals, Kyberna, generalists and fitness.
   - **Local AI:** who gets cited in llama.cpp issue threads, and whom Willison tags.
   - **Japan:** 稲葉's public Hatena bookmarks and the note follow lists of hub thinkers.
   - **Theory:** co-authorship and journal publication (Berry co-wrote with Galloway; Technophany published Roden).
   - **JDM:** almost no graph. The practitioner who cites NHTSA/CARB documents and whom Hagerty interviews by name.
3. **Count endorsements from unrelated seeds.** Cliques that recommend each other count once (Farrell–Recht–Davies–Munger; the Holmer–Luks–Byrn–Couzens endurance cluster).
4. **Cut hard.** These removed the most candidates:
   - paywalls (the best-connected fitness writers paywall 35–75%)
   - silence of 60–120+ days (Karpathy, Carlini)
   - posting only on X, which has no feed
   - selling courses, supplements or apps
   - AI-generated posts (樋口恭介, Yassine)
   - fame with no graph edge (内田樹)
5. **Check paywalls post by post**, using the Substack audience field and the note API. Re-verify every claim: the skeptic pass found inflated counts.

**Each quarter:** re-pull your best people's recommendation lists. Anyone recommended by two of them is the next candidate.

## Paywalls

Ian subscribes to nothing, so no paywalled articles. Removed: The Kaitchup (Benjamin Marie): 12 of its 20 feed items and 16 of its last 30 posts are paid-only, and those are its core quantization and benchmark posts. Only the weekly roundups are free, so it fails the "(nearly) all free" bar even with a filter.; Japan Times: news and commentary sit behind the Piano paywall (3 of 4 sampled pages lacked the 'nopaywall' tag). The feed carries no marker, so a cheap filter isn't possible.; 千葉雅也（note）: all 25 feed items are paid, either 200 yen each or magazine/membership-only. The note API re-check today on n48f3b53ab843 returned price 200, can_read false.; Deleuze and Guattari Studies: OpenAlex shows all 11 items in the current issue as closed access, including the LLM paper. Only the abstracts are free.. Also removed: Theory, Culture & Society (mixed open access; SAGE pages block the page check). Item-level filters in `aggregator/fetch.py`: Substack paid posts (their content ends in a bare "Read more" link — 13/13 paid, 0/37 free), links to known paywalled outlets (`PAYWALLED_HOSTS`, incl. Google News' `<source>`), and a page check (`isAccessibleForFree` in the JSON-LD, fail closed) for note.com items and feeds marked `check_access` (Nature). PubMed abstracts are free and stay.

## Volume

**Volume.** The plan adds 35 feeds: 4 local AI, 6 research agents, 5 Deleuze, 3 fitness, 4 Japan, 2 JDM, 5 Kyberna and 6 generalist. That fits the ~6-per-lane cap. Davies, Recht, Munger and Hamel each came up in 2-3 lanes and are added once; that overlap is itself a strong signal.
- The 33 uncapped feeds post about 22.7 items a week combined.
- 濱口 (7.5/wk) and Gelman (10.8/wk) are capped at max_items 1 per run.
- Total is about 37 a week, or roughly 5 candidate items a day. That is well under the +25/day budget, and the brief still picks at most 12.
- It shrinks further because of the four removals (Japan Times is a daily news firehose) and because rule A drops about 5 paid items a week from existing feeds (Latent Space AINews dailies, Construction Physics Reading Lists, Interconnects and Holmer paid posts).

**Feeds where the picker sees little text.** Weights are set with this in mind:
- Gordić: titles only
- Litt: ~240-char excerpts
- 筒井 (note): teasers
- ジュエルオート: ~150-char teasers
- Hamel: 8 of 20 items are short stubs

**Hamel link collision.** 7 of Hamel's 20 items share the link https://hamel.dev/. dedupe_links (fetch.py:313) and the seen-URL history (triage.py:741) both key on the link, so they will merge or suppress his items. Key them on guid, or on link+title.
